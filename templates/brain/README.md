# Brain Template

Copy this entire `brain/` directory into your workspace to start an institutional brain for a domain (for example, `my-team/brain/`). Keep `templates/brain/` itself clean.

## Before you start
1. Fill in `config.yaml` (owner, backup reviewer, review windows, sensitivity levels).
2. Run **`skills/build/SKILL.md`** to define your Lenses (`lens/`), register sources (`record/`), seed definitions (`business-understanding/`), backfill history, and interview your expert.
3. **Never edit memory files directly.** All changes flow through `proposals/` and are applied by the single applier in `skills/learn/SKILL.md`.

---

## Strict boundaries: What goes where (no open-ended dumping)

Every piece of knowledge has **exactly one home**. Use this test before putting anything into a folder:

| Folder | What it is for | What deserves to be here | What does NOT belong here |
|---|---|---|---|
| `lens/` (`LENS-`) | **The job.** Recurring deliverables and decisions. | Has a recurring cadence, named audience, decision supported, `governing_question`, and `completion_rule`. | Broad topics (*"Finance"*), one-off questions, or raw datasets. |
| `record/` (`REC-`) | **The truth.** Pointers to docs, tables, tools, and source rankings. | Original artifacts, tool contracts (`can_establish` / `cannot_establish`), and source-authority rules. | Copied numbers, summaries, or interpretations. |
| `business-understanding/` (`BU-`) | **The mechanics.** Terms, formulas, normal ranges, metric links. | Still true next period unless the business or accounting rules change. | Performance conclusions that new data could disprove. |
| `beliefs/` (`BEL-`) | **The patterns.** Multi-event insights and open Story Threads. | Synthesizes 2+ `BU-` entries, says what no single doc says, and implies an action for a `LENS-`. | Single-period facts or metric definitions. |
| `reasoning-patterns/` (`RP-`) | **The thinking.** How experts investigate and when they stop. | Question sequences or judgment rules seen in 2+ independent events (or ratified by an expert). | Rigid commands or unratified guesses. |
| `editorial/` (`ED-`) | **The voice.** How deliverables are structured per audience. | Lead, include, and omit rules seen in 2+ deliverables (or ratified by the owner). | Analysis logic or data definitions. |

## Governance folders & files
- `proposals/` — Pending memory changes (`<ULID>.yaml`); `pending/` awaits human approval, `rejected/` failed validation or was declined.
- `passes/` — Working scratchpad for `Learn` runs (`LP-<ULID>/`).
- `archive/` — Retired or expired entries kept for audit history (excluded from `index.yaml`).
- `config.yaml` — Ownership, review windows, and sensitivity levels.
- `index.yaml` — Auto-generated lookup map (never hand-edit; regenerated by `schemas/validate.py`).
- `match_ledger.yaml` — Audit log of how every `Learn` signal was routed.
- `changelog.md` — Append-only log of all applied changes.
